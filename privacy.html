Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

Olive Path Network ("we", "us", or "our"), operated by EBroni Global Media, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Olive App mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and password when you create an account.
  • Google Sign-In Data: If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email, and profile picture.
  • Prayer Requests: Content you submit through the prayer request feature.
  • Booking Information: Name, phone, email, and details you provide when booking counselling or pastor programs.
  • Payment Information: Transactions are processed by Paystack. We do not store your card details.
  • Usage Data: App interactions, pages viewed, and content accessed.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the app's services
  • To manage your account and provide access to content
  • To process book purchases and payments
  • To respond to prayer requests and booking submissions
  • To send notifications about new teachings, devotions, and programs
  • To improve our app and user experience

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Paystack: For payment processing
  • Google: For authentication via Google Sign-In
  • Ministry Team: Prayer requests and booking submissions are shared with the Olive Path ministry team

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS), secure password hashing, and JWT-based authentication.

5. Your Rights

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Delete your account by contacting us

6. Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
